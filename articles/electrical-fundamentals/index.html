Frequently Asked Questions

What should I learn first in electrical engineering?

Start with voltage, current and resistance. Once these concepts are clear, learn Ohm's Law and electrical power before moving into more advanced circuit and power-system topics.

Why are voltage, current and resistance important?

They describe some of the most fundamental electrical relationships in a circuit. Ohm's Law connects these quantities through V = I × R.

Is this section suitable for beginners?

Yes. The tutorials are intended to build the fundamentals step by step before progressing to more advanced electrical engineering subjects.
