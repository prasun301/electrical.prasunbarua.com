Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua, why it may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors of Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua at prasunbarua.com.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua.

This website is an independent educational and technical resource covering electrical engineering, renewable energy, solar photovoltaic systems, power systems, electrical design, engineering calculations and related technical subjects.

I respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be collected and used when you visit this website.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected, how that information may be used, cookies and similar technologies, advertising services, analytics, third-party services, and the choices available to visitors.

2. Information We May Collect

The website is primarily an informational resource. You can generally browse and read the content without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

Depending on how you interact with the website, certain information may nevertheless be processed automatically or voluntarily provided by you.

Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact me through a contact form, email, or another communication method provided on the website, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

This information is used primarily to respond to your communication and address your request.

Information collected automatically

When you visit a website, certain technical information may be automatically transmitted by your browser or device. Depending on the services enabled on this website, this may include information such as:

  • IP address or an approximate location derived from it;
  • browser type and version;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • referring website or page;
  • pages viewed;
  • approximate time spent on pages;
  • date and time of access;
  • general interaction and usage information;
  • technical information required to maintain website security and functionality.

Much of this information is technical or statistical in nature and is not intentionally used by this website to identify individual visitors personally.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information collected through the website may be used for legitimate purposes such as:

  • operating and maintaining the website;
  • improving website performance and usability;
  • understanding which content is useful to visitors;
  • analyzing general website traffic and usage;
  • detecting, preventing and investigating security issues or abuse;
  • responding to questions and messages;
  • displaying and measuring advertising;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations.

I do not intentionally collect personal information simply because you visit or read an article on this website.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

This website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and its advertising partners.

Google and third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers or similar technologies to help serve, measure and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable settings, consent requirements and advertising configuration, ads may be personalized based on information such as a visitor's previous interactions with websites or other online activity.

Google states that advertising cookies may be used to help serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Advertising choice

Visitors may manage or turn off personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings. Choosing to disable personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that advertisements will no longer be displayed. Instead, advertising may be less personalized or based on contextual information.

Google also provides information about how its advertising technologies work and how users can manage advertising preferences.

Google and its advertising partners may operate independently from this website and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Google Analytics

If Google Analytics is enabled on this website, it may be used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content, structure and performance.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate geographic information, device and browser information, traffic sources, and interactions with the website.

Analytics information is generally used in aggregated or statistical form to understand website performance rather than to identify individual visitors.

If Analytics is disabled or removed from the website in the future, this section will no longer apply to that service.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website and certain third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies.

A cookie is a small text file stored by a website in your browser. Cookies can perform different functions, including remembering preferences, supporting website functionality, measuring traffic, preventing abuse, and helping deliver or measure advertising.

Types of cookies that may be used

Essential or functional technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to function correctly, maintain security, remember certain preferences, or provide requested functionality.

Analytics technologies

Analytics technologies may help understand how visitors use the website, which pages are visited, and how the website performs.

Advertising technologies

Advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies and similar technologies to display, measure, limit or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or experience of some websites.

7. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Advertising displayed through Google services may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable laws, consent, user settings and the advertising configuration of the website.

Personalized advertising may use information associated with previous browsing activity or interactions to make advertisements more relevant.

Non-personalized advertising generally relies more heavily on contextual information, such as the content of the page being viewed, rather than a user's previous browsing behavior.

Where legally required, appropriate consent mechanisms may be used before certain cookies or personal information are processed for advertising purposes.
