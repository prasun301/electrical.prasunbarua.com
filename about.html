About this website

Practical electrical engineering, explained clearly.

Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua is an independent engineering knowledge platform created to make electrical engineering, power systems, renewable energy and technical concepts easier to understand and apply.

Why this website exists

Electrical engineering is a field where a small misunderstanding can lead to a large practical problem. A formula may look simple on paper, while its real-world application can involve design assumptions, equipment limitations, safety requirements, environmental conditions and engineering judgment.

This website was created with a simple objective: to explain engineering concepts in a way that is technically meaningful, practical and easy to follow.

Rather than focusing only on definitions, the articles aim to connect theory with engineering practice. Wherever appropriate, concepts are explained through calculations, examples, diagrams, design considerations and real-world applications.

What you will find here

The website covers a growing range of topics related to electrical engineering and energy systems.

  • Electrical engineering fundamentals
  • Electrical power systems
  • Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems
  • Renewable energy technologies
  • Electrical design and calculations
  • Power generation and distribution
  • Electrical equipment and components
  • Engineering formulas and practical examples
  • Solar PV system design considerations
  • Technical explanations for students, engineers and professionals

Engineering should be understood, not memorized

One of the principles behind this website is that engineering knowledge becomes much more useful when you understand why something works, rather than simply memorizing a formula.

For example, knowing the equation for electrical power is only the beginning. An engineer also needs to understand how voltage, current, power factor, efficiency, loading, conductor capacity, equipment ratings and operating conditions affect an actual system.

The same principle applies to renewable energy. A solar PV system is more than a collection of modules and inverters. Proper design requires an understanding of solar resource, system losses, module characteristics, inverter limits, electrical protection, cable sizing, energy yield and site conditions.

The goal here is therefore not simply to provide an answer, but to explain the engineering thinking behind the answer.

Our approach to technical content

Technical information should be useful, but it should also be responsible.

Articles are written with an emphasis on clarity, logical explanation and practical engineering context. Calculations are presented step by step where they help explain the underlying concept, and technical assumptions are identified whenever they are important to the result.

Engineering standards, manufacturer specifications and project requirements can vary. For that reason, information published on this website should be treated as educational and general technical guidance rather than a substitute for project-specific engineering design, professional review or applicable codes and standards.

Accuracy matters

Engineering content deserves a higher standard than ordinary internet content. A calculation, specification or technical recommendation can influence an actual engineering decision.

For that reason, the aim is to keep articles technically sound, clearly written and transparent about assumptions and limitations.

When equipment specifications or technical parameters are discussed, readers should always verify the latest information from the relevant manufacturer, applicable standards and project documentation before using it for an actual installation or engineering decision.

An independent engineering resource

Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua is an independently created educational website. Its purpose is to share engineering knowledge, practical experience and useful technical explanations with readers around the world.

The website is not intended to replace formal engineering education. Instead, it is designed to complement it — providing another place where concepts can be explored, practical questions can be examined and technical knowledge can be revisited.

Engineering is a continuous learning process

Electrical engineering continues to evolve. Renewable energy, energy storage, power electronics, smart grids, digital monitoring and modern electrical technologies are changing the way engineers design and operate energy systems.

This website will continue to grow alongside those developments, with new articles and explanations added over time.

The objective is simple: make useful engineering knowledge easier to understand, easier to revisit and easier to apply responsibly.
