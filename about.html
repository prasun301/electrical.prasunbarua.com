About Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua

Electrical engineering is a field where theoretical knowledge and practical application are closely connected. Understanding a formula is important, but knowing when, why and how to apply it is equally important.

Electrical Engineering by Prasun Barua was created with this idea in mind. The site provides practical explanations, engineering calculations, technical guides and educational resources designed to help students, technicians, engineers and anyone interested in electrical technology build a stronger understanding of the subject.

The goal is not simply to present formulas or technical definitions. Instead, the content aims to explain engineering concepts in a straightforward way, show how calculations work and provide useful context for real-world applications.

Our Approach

Good engineering education should be understandable without becoming oversimplified. This site therefore aims to maintain a balance between technical accuracy and clear communication.

Where appropriate, articles use equations, examples, tables and step-by-step calculations to explain a subject. The intention is to help readers understand the reasoning behind a calculation rather than simply providing a final number.

Engineering practice also requires professional judgement. Actual projects may involve applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, site conditions, environmental factors, protection coordination, utility requirements and other project-specific considerations.

For that reason, information published on this website should be treated as educational and reference material rather than a substitute for project-specific engineering design, verification or professional review.
