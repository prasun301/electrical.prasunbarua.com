ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Practical Engineering, Clearly Explained.

Electrical Engineering is an independent educational resource created to make electrical engineering, calculations, power systems, and solar PV concepts easier to understand and apply in practice.

PB
person ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Prasun Barua

I am a Senior Electrical Engineer with more than 18 years of professional experience in electrical engineering, renewable energy, and solar photovoltaic projects.

My professional work has included electrical design, engineering calculations, equipment selection, testing and commissioning, as well as the development and implementation of grid-connected solar PV projects.

I created this website to share some of the engineering knowledge, practical methods, and lessons that are useful to students, engineers, technicians, and anyone interested in understanding how electrical systems work.

lightbulb MY APPROACH

Understand. Calculate. Apply.

Good engineering learning should go beyond memorising formulas. Each subject should help the reader understand what is happening, how to calculate it, and where the result is useful.

01 · UNDERSTAND
psychology

Understand the Principle

Begin with the physical meaning of the concept, the important terminology, and the engineering principle behind it.

02 · CALCULATE
functions

Work Through the Numbers

Apply appropriate formulas, assumptions, and units through clear worked examples so the calculation can be followed and checked.

03 · APPLY

Educational Resource, Not Project Approval

The information on this website is provided for general educational and informational purposes. Engineering decisions for actual projects should be based on applicable standards, local regulations, project specifications, manufacturer documentation, site conditions, and appropriate professional engineering review.

Calculators and examples published here are intended to support learning and preliminary analysis. They should not be treated as a substitute for detailed project engineering, inspection, testing, or professional approval.
