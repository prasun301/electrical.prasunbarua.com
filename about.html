About Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering is an educational resource focused on making electrical engineering concepts easier to understand through practical explanations, calculations and real-world engineering examples.

About Prasun Barua

I am Prasun Barua, a Senior Electrical Engineer with professional experience in renewable energy, utility-scale solar PV projects, electrical design, testing and commissioning.

My professional experience includes electrical engineering work associated with solar PV power plants and electrical systems across different international projects.

Through this website, I share electrical engineering knowledge in a practical and accessible format for students, engineers, technicians and anyone interested in understanding how electrical systems work.

Our Approach

The goal is not simply to provide formulas. Tutorials are designed to explain why a calculation is performed, how the formula works, and how the result can be interpreted in a practical engineering context.

Where appropriate, articles include worked examples, engineering notes, diagrams and links to useful calculation tools.

Important Note

The information published on this website is provided for educational and informational purposes. Engineering decisions for actual projects should be verified against applicable standards, project specifications, manufacturer documentation and the judgment of a qualified professional.
